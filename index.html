Moving Tips & Checklist

Follow these steps for a smooth, stress-free move.

  • Download our household inventory spreadsheet and fill it in
  • Contact Dude & Van to check availability and get a quote
  • Start decluttering — donate or sell items you no longer need
  • Notify your landlord and arrange end-of-lease inspection
  • Arrange storage if there's a gap between move-out and move-in
  • Order boxes & packaging materials from us
  • Start packing non-essentials: books, art, seasonal items
  • Label every box with room AND contents
  • Wrap fragile items with bubble wrap — pack heavier items at the bottom
  • Send photos or a video walkthrough to our team via WhatsApp
  • Ensure parking is reserved for the van at both properties
  • Pack a "day bag" with essentials (chargers, toiletries, snacks)
  • Do a final walkthrough — check every room, cupboard & garage
  • Let our team leader supervise the load — every job has one!
  • Track your van live via GPS — we'll keep you updated throughout
  • Check all items against your inventory before the team leaves
  • Reassemble furniture — our team can assist with this too
  • Update your address with banks, home affairs & subscriptions
  • Transfer your water, electricity & internet accounts
  • Leave us a Google review if we made your day! ⭐
